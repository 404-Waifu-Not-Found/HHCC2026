# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in ClipQuest welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of behavior.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work.
- Respect different backgrounds, perspectives, and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially where possible.
